# Remaining SEO Opportunities

1. **Dedicated product pages**  
   If product data becomes available locally instead of only through Printify, add first-class `/products/$slug` routes with unique metadata, reviews, availability, and canonical product URLs.

2. **Generated Open Graph images**  
   Social cards currently reuse artwork images. A future enhancement could generate cropped OG images with artwork titles and the GAK Creations brand treatment.

3. **Search and filtering**  
   If the catalogue grows, a searchable archive page could support long-tail keyword capture around medium, location, and collection intent.

4. **Analytics-driven content refresh**  
   Monitor landing-page performance in Search Console and analytics, then expand journal content around the strongest collection and query themes.

5. **Merchant and review schema**  
   If verified review or pricing data becomes available, extend product schema with aggregate ratings and more precise offer data.
